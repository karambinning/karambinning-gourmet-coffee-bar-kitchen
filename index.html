Section II · The Six-Point Protocol

Six things we won't compromise on.

Every cup served on a platform under our name passes through these six checks. They're the difference between a coffee and a speciality coffee, and the reason our networks trust us with their busiest concourses.

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Bought direct from named farms in Brazil. Long relationships, fair prices paid above the C-market, lots cupped before they ever cross the Atlantic.

  2. 02

    Grading

    Speciality grade by SCA standard — 80 points and above. Defect counts, screen sizes and moisture all verified at intake. If a lot doesn't pass, it doesn't enter the roast schedule.

  3. 03

    Roasting

    Small-batch profile roasting at our Wrexham roastery. The profile is matched to the bean, not the other way around. Every batch cupped on the day of roast.

  4. 04

    Flavour

    Bright, chocolatey, balanced — the cup you want at 6:48 a.m. with a suitcase at your ankles. Distinct enough to notice, never so loud it argues with you.

  5. 05

    Perfectly served

    Pulled on La Marzocco by trained baristas, milk steamed properly, every ticket timed. The kit and the people both held to the same standard.

  6. 06

    Transparency & traceability

    You can ask which farm your cup came from and we can tell you. Lot codes printed on every retail bag, full origin reports available to network partners.
